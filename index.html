Philosophy

How we think about building tools

Privacy first

None of our tools collect your data. What stays on your device, stays on your device. Always.

Free forever

Every product in the utilsware family is free — for personal use, for commercial use, for any use. That's a commitment, not a promotion.

Built for speed

Developer tools should disappear into your workflow. We obsess over performance so our tools never slow you down.

Focused quality

One developer, deliberate choices. Every feature in every product earns its place. No bloat, no filler.
