Frequently asked questions

Is Type-Vantage really free?›

Yes. The full typing course is free on the web, on desktop, and on mobile. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no paywalls.

Do I need to install anything?›

No. You can take the entire course right in your browser at type-vantage.utilsware.com — nothing to download. If you'd prefer a native, offline-friendly experience, you can also install the Windows, macOS, or Linux desktop app or the Android app.

Which desktop platforms are supported?›

Type-Vantage ships native desktop apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Windows comes as an .exe installer plus a portable .zip; macOS as a .dmg; Linux as .AppImage and .deb packages. This page automatically highlights the download for whichever system you're browsing from.

Where do I download the desktop and mobile apps?›

Every installer and the Android APK download directly from utilsware — just pick your platform in the Download section on this page and the file starts downloading immediately. The same builds are also attached to each GitHub release if you'd rather browse previous versions.

Do I need an account?›

No accounts, ever. Your profiles, progress, and settings are stored locally on the device you're practising on. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to remember.

Is it suitable for children?›

Absolutely. Type-Vantage has an Explorer tier tuned for ages 5–8 with short, playful words and gentle stories, plus tiers for older kids, teens, and adults. Because it collects no personal data and shows no ads, it's a comfortable choice for classrooms and homes alike.

How is my typing measured?›

Type-Vantage shows live words-per-minute and accuracy as you type, and awards up to three stars per chapter based on your speed and precision. Chapters unlock in sequence, so you always have a clear next step.

Does it work offline?›

The desktop and mobile apps are built to run fully offline once installed. The web version needs a connection to load the first time, after which lessons run smoothly in the browser.

Can it teach me to type code?›

Yes. Alongside words, sentences, and stories, the Expert Arena includes realistic code snippets and symbol-heavy passages, so developers can sharpen speed on the characters that matter most.

Is my data private?›

Yes. Type-Vantage stores everything locally and sends none of your keystrokes or progress to any server. There are no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.

Ready to leave hunt-and-peck behind?

Open Type-Vantage in your browser and start in seconds — or install it on Windows, Linux, and Android for offline practice. It's free, and there's nothing to sign up for.
