Frequently asked questions

Is Arcadami really free?›

Yes. Every game is free to play on the web, on desktop, and on mobile. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no paywalls.

Do I need to install anything?›

No. You can play the full collection right in your browser at arcadami.utilsware.com — nothing to download. If you'd prefer a native, offline-friendly experience, you can also install the Windows, macOS, or Linux desktop app or the Android app.

Which desktop platforms are supported?›

Arcadami ships native desktop apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Windows comes as an .exe installer plus a portable .zip; macOS as a .dmg; Linux as .AppImage and .deb packages. This page automatically highlights the download for whichever system you're browsing from.

Where do I download the desktop and mobile apps?›

Every installer and the Android APK download directly from utilsware — just pick your platform in the Downloads section on this page and the file starts downloading immediately. The same builds are also attached to each GitHub release if you'd rather browse previous versions.

Do I need an account?›

No accounts, ever. Your profiles, scores, and settings are stored locally on the device you're playing on. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to remember.

Is it suitable for children?›

Absolutely. Arcadami is designed to be family-friendly — bright, approachable, and safe. Because it collects no personal data and shows no ads, it's a comfortable choice for kids, and many games are great to play together.

Does it work offline?›

The desktop and mobile apps are built to run offline once installed. The web version needs a connection to load the first time, after which most games play smoothly in the browser.

What kinds of games are included?›

66 games across ten categories: Memory (Memory Match, What's Missing, Melody Memory), Concentration (Stroop, Whack-a-Mole, Reaction Duel, Rhythm Tap), Logic (Sudoku, Nonogram, Mastermind, Sokoban, Pattern Cracker), Strategy (Chess, Checkers, Backgammon, Dominoes, Connect Four, Tic Tac Toe), Spatial (Maze Runner, Tangram, 15 Puzzle, Rush Hour, Jigsaw), Math (Math Sprint, Times Tables, Fraction Frenzy, Estimation Station), Language (Word Search, Mini Crossword, Hangman, Word Ladder, Anagram Attack), Knowledge (Quizzes, Flags & Capitals, Logo Quiz, Silhouette Guess), Magic (The Mind Reader, Card Force, Birthday Mystery), and Casual & Fun (Ludo, Carrom, Snake & Ladder, Bingo, Marble Drop).

Is my data private?›

Yes. Arcadami stores everything locally and sends none of your gameplay or profiles to any server. There are no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.

Ready to play?

Open Arcadami in your browser and start in seconds — or install it on Windows, Linux, and Android for offline fun. It's free, and there's nothing to sign up for.
